FAQ

Is this safe? Will I get action-blocked?

Insta-Unfollow uses conservative default settings (40/hr, 200/day) to minimize risk. It detects action blocks automatically and stops. We recommend starting with conservative settings and only increasing if your account handles it well.

Do I need to give you my Instagram password?

No. You log in through Instagram's real login page in a browser window. Your credentials never leave your machine. We have no access to your account.

What's the difference between free and paid?

The free version (GitHub) requires Node.js and running a command. The $9.99 Chrome extension is the same tool but with one-click install — no terminal, no setup. Same features, same safety.

Does this work on Mac?

Yes. The Chrome extension works on any OS with Chrome. The open-source version works on Windows, Mac, and Linux with Node.js.

Can I pause and resume?

Yes. You can pause, resume, or stop at any time. Progress is saved after every unfollow, so you never lose progress.

What if I get a "Confirm it's you" prompt?

Stop immediately and wait 24-48 hours. This is Instagram's anti-automation check. The tool will detect it and stop automatically.
