Settings

Recommended Settings to Avoid Action Blocks

  • Conservative: Hourly: 40-60, Daily: 200-400, Delay: 8-15s
  • Moderate: Hourly: 80-120, Daily: 500-800, Delay: 5-10s
  • Aggressive (risky): Hourly: 150-200, Daily: 800-1200, Delay: 3-6s
  • Always take breaks every 50-80 unfollows (1-3 min)
  • New/young accounts: stay under 50/hr and 200/day
  • If Instagram shows a "Confirm it's you" prompt, STOP immediately
  • Don't unfollow more than 1,000 accounts per day total
  • Spread unfollowing across multiple sessions, not one long run
  • Accounts under 1 year old should use conservative settings
